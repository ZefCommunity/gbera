Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about payments, cancellations, safety, and lost items.

  • What payment methods are accepted? We accept cash, bank transfers, and supported card payments where available.
  • When am I charged? You’ll be charged after your trip ends, based on the fare shown in the app or agreed at completion.
  • Why was I charged twice? If you see a duplicate charge, it may be a pending authorization. It usually clears within a few banking hours. If it persists, contact support with your trip details.
  • Can I get a receipt? Yes. Receipts are sent to your email or can be viewed in your Ride History.
  • How do I cancel a ride? You can cancel from the booking screen or trip tracking screen before the driver marks the trip as started.
  • Is there a cancellation fee? A fee may apply if you cancel after the driver has been assigned and has started heading to your pickup location, depending on local policies.
  • Can the driver cancel? Yes. If a driver cancels, we’ll help you find another available driver as soon as possible.
  • What if my ride was cancelled unfairly? Report it via “Report a problem with a trip” below with details and we’ll review.
  • How do I stay safe during a trip? Share your trip details with a trusted contact, verify the driver and vehicle match your booking, and sit in the back seat where safe to do so.
  • What should I do in an emergency? Use your local emergency number first, then report the incident to Gbera support immediately with your trip ID and location.
  • Are drivers screened? Drivers go through identity verification and background checks in line with our safety policy.
  • Can I rate my driver? Yes. After each trip, you’ll have the opportunity to rate and leave feedback.
  • What if I left something in the vehicle? Use “Report a problem with a trip” below, select “Lost item”, and include your trip details and a description of the item.
  • How will I get it back? We’ll contact the driver on your behalf. If they find the item, we’ll coordinate a safe return. Return fees may apply depending on the situation.
  • How quickly should I report? Report as soon as possible — the sooner we contact the driver, the higher the chance of recovery.
  • What if I can’t reach support immediately? Check your email and the trip details in Ride History for updates or driver contact instructions if provided.
